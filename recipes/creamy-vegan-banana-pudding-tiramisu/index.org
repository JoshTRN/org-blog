#+title: Creamy Vegan Banana Pudding Tiramisu
#+include: /home/joshua/org/blog/recipes/header.org

+ *Prep Time*: /~30 min/
+ *Cook Time*: /~0 min/
+ *Servings*: /~8/
+ *Cost per Serving*: /~$2.25/
+ *Total Cost*: /~$18.00/

* Ingredients
** Banana Pudding
+ [ ] 3 ripe bananas (mashed)
+ [ ] 1 1/2 cups oat milk
+ [ ] 1/2 cup coconut cream
+ [ ] 1/3 cup organic cane sugar
+ [ ] 3 tbsp cornstarch
+ [ ] 1 tsp vanilla extract
+ [ ] Pinch of salt

** Tiramisu Cream
+ [ ] 1 1/2 cups vegan cream cheese
+ [ ] 1 cup coconut cream
+ [ ] 1/2 cup powdered sugar
+ [ ] 1 tsp vanilla extract

** Assembly
+ [ ] 2 cups strong brewed coffee (cooled)
+ [ ] 20-24 vegan vanilla wafers or ladyfingers
+ [ ] 2 bananas (sliced thin)
+ [ ] 2 tbsp cocoa powder (for dusting)

* Method
** Banana Pudding
1. [ ] In a saucepan over medium heat, whisk together oat milk, coconut cream, sugar, cornstarch, and salt until smooth.
2. [ ] Cook, stirring constantly, until mixture thickens (~5-7 minutes).
3. [ ] Remove from heat and stir in mashed bananas and vanilla extract. Let cool completely.

** Tiramisu Cream
1. [ ] In a large bowl, beat vegan cream cheese and coconut cream until smooth.
2. [ ] Add powdered sugar and vanilla, then beat until fluffy.
3. [ ] Chill until ready to use.

** Assembly
1. [ ] Briefly dip each wafer into the cooled coffee and layer on the bottom of a 9x13 inch dish.
2. [ ] Spread half the banana pudding over the wafers, followed by half the tiramisu cream.
3. [ ] Add a layer of sliced bananas.
4. [ ] Repeat with another layer of dipped wafers, banana pudding, tiramisu cream, and bananas.
5. [ ] Dust the top generously with cocoa powder.
6. [ ] Chill in the refrigerator for at least 4 hours or overnight before serving.

* Notes
+ Use very ripe bananas for maximum sweetness and flavor.
+ Can be made a day in advance for best texture.
+ Substitute espresso for a stronger coffee flavor.
