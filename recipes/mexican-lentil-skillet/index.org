#+title: Mexican Lentil Skillet
#+INCLUDE: /home/joshua/org/blog/recipes/header.org
+ *Prep Time*: /~15 min/
+ *Cook Time*: /~35 min/
+ *Servings*: /~6/
+ *Cost per Serving*: /~$1.75/
+ *Total Cost*: /~$10.00/

* Ingredients
** Base
- [ ] 1 tbsp olive oil
- [ ] 1/2 white onion (diced)
- [ ] 1 large sweet potato (peeled, 1/2 inch dice)
- [ ] 1 tbsp minced garlic
- [ ] 1 cup dry lentils (rinsed)
- [ ] 1 tbsp tomato paste
- [ ] 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes
- [ ] 3 cups vegetable broth
- [ ] 1 can (15 oz) black beans (drained, rinsed)
- [ ] 1 can (15 oz) corn (drained)

** Seasoning & Finish
- [ ] 1 1/2 tbsp taco seasoning
- [ ] Black pepper (to taste)
- [ ] 1 cup vegan cheddar (shredded)
- [ ] Cilantro (chopped, for topping)

* Method
1. [ ] Heat oil; sauté onion, sweet potato, and garlic until softened.
2. [ ] Stir in tomato paste and taco seasoning; cook 1 minute.
3. [ ] Add tomatoes, broth, lentils, black beans, and corn; simmer covered ~30 minutes, stirring.
4. [ ] Top with vegan cheddar to melt. Finish with black pepper and cilantro.

* Notes
- [ ] Serve with lime wedges and avocado.
- [ ] Use red lentils for faster cooking (reduce liquid slightly).
