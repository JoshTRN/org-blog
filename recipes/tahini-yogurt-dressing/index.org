#+title: Tahini Yogurt Dressing
#+INCLUDE: /home/joshua/org/blog/recipes/header.org
+ *Prep Time*: /~5 min/
+ *Cook Time*: /~0 min/
+ *Servings*: /~8/
+ *Cost per Serving*: /~$0.25/
+ *Total Cost*: /~$2.75/

* Ingredients
- [ ] 2 tbsp tahini
- [ ] 120 g vegan yogurt (plain, unsweetened)
- [ ] 2 tbsp lemon juice
- [ ] 1 clove garlic (minced or grated)
- [ ] 2 tbsp olive oil
- [ ] 1 tsp mustard
- [ ] 1 tsp agave
- [ ] 1/4 tsp salt
- [ ] 1/8 tsp black pepper

* Method
1. [ ] Whisk all ingredients until smooth. Thin with a splash of water if needed.

* Notes
- Add chopped herbs (parsley, dill, cilantro) for variation.
