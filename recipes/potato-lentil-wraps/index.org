#+title: Potato Lentil Wraps (Burritos)
#+INCLUDE: /home/joshua/org/blog/recipes/header.org
+ *Prep Time*: /~15 min/
+ *Cook Time*: /~30 min/
+ *Servings*: /~6/
+ *Cost per Serving*: /~$1.25/
+ *Total Cost*: /~$7.00/

* Ingredients
- [ ] 1 tbsp olive oil
- [ ] 1/2 onion (diced)
- [ ] 1/2 lb dry lentils (rinsed)
- [ ] 3 large russet potatoes (peeled, diced)
- [ ] 3 1/2 cups water or vegetable broth
- [ ] 1 tsp salt
- [ ] 1 tsp black pepper
- [ ] 1 tsp onion powder
- [ ] 1 tsp garlic powder
- [ ] 1 tsp oregano
- [ ] 1 tsp chili powder
- [ ] 1 tsp cumin
- [ ] 1 tsp paprika
- [ ] 6 large tortillas
- [ ] Oil for crisping (optional)

* Method
1. [ ] Sauté onion in oil until tender. Add garlic powder and spices; toast 30 seconds.
2. [ ] Add potatoes, water/broth, and lentils; simmer ~25 minutes until lentils are tender.
3. [ ] Fill tortillas and roll tightly. Optional: pan-crisp with a little oil.

* Notes
- [ ] Add salsa or avocado for freshness.
- [ ] Batch-freeze assembled wraps before crisping.
