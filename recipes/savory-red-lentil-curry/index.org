#+title: Savory Red Lentil & Chickpea Curry (No Ginger)
#+INCLUDE: /home/joshua/org/blog/recipes/header.org
+ *Prep Time*: /~15 min/
+ *Cook Time*: /~25 min/
+ *Servings*: /~6/
+ *Cost per Serving*: /~$1.50/
+ *Total Cost*: /~$8.50/

* Ingredients
- [ ] 1 cup red lentils (rinsed)
- [ ] 1 can (15 oz) chickpeas (drained, rinsed)
- [ ] 1 large onion (finely diced)
- [ ] 3 cloves garlic (minced)
- [ ] 2 tbsp neutral oil
- [ ] 1 tbsp tomato paste
- [ ] 1 can (14 oz) chopped tomatoes
- [ ] 1 can (14 oz) full-fat coconut milk
- [ ] 2 1/2 cups vegetable broth
- [ ] 2 tbsp soy sauce or tamari
- [ ] 1 tbsp nutritional yeast
- [ ] 1 tsp mushroom powder
- [ ] 1 tsp curry powder
- [ ] 1/2 tsp ground coriander
- [ ] 1/4 tsp turmeric
- [ ] 1 tsp smoked paprika
- [ ] 1/2 tsp black pepper
- [ ] 1/2 tsp chili flakes (optional)
- [ ] Juice of 1/2 lemon
- [ ] Salt (to taste)
- [ ] Fresh cilantro or scallions (for garnish)

* Method
1. [ ] Sauté onion in oil until deeply golden; add garlic 1 minute.
2. [ ] Stir in tomato paste; cook until darkened.
3. [ ] Add spices; toast briefly until fragrant.
4. [ ] Add tomatoes, soy, nutritional yeast, coconut milk, broth, lentils, chickpeas, and mushroom powder.
5. [ ] Simmer 20–25 minutes until lentils are soft and curry thickens.
6. [ ] Finish with lemon juice; adjust salt. Garnish with herbs.

* Notes
- [ ] Serve with basmati rice or flatbread.
