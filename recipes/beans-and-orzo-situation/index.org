#+title: Beans and Orzo Situation
#+INCLUDE: /home/joshua/org/blog/recipes/header.org
+ *Prep Time*: /~15 min/
+ *Cook Time*: /~20 min/
+ *Servings*: /~4/
+ *Cost per Serving*: /~$1.75/
+ *Total Cost*: /~$7.50/

* Ingredients
- [ ] 2 tbsp olive oil
- [ ] 1 onion (diced)
- [ ] 1 carrot (diced)
- [ ] 1 celery stalk (diced)
- [ ] 1 broccoli (stem diced, florets small)
- [ ] 3 cloves garlic (minced)
- [ ] 250 g orzo (or 150 g rice)
- [ ] 1 L vegetable stock
- [ ] 1 can (400 g) beans (cannellini or chickpeas; drained)
- [ ] 20 g nutritional yeast
- [ ] 1 lemon (juiced)
- [ ] Salt (to taste)

* Method
** Build & Simmer
1. [ ] Sauté onion, carrot, celery, and broccoli stem in oil 7–8 minutes until soft.
2. [ ] Add garlic and orzo; toast 30 seconds.
3. [ ] Pour in stock and beans; simmer 8–10 minutes until orzo is tender and broth thickens.

** Finish
1. [ ] Stir in nutritional yeast and lemon juice. Season to taste and serve.

* Notes
- [ ] Fold in spinach or kale in the last 2 minutes.
- [ ] Chili flakes add a nice kick.
