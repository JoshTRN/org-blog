#+title: Vegan Tuscan-Style Cutlets
#+include: /home/joshua/org/blog/recipes/header.org
+ *Prep Time*: /~20 min/
+ *Cook Time*: /~35 min/
+ *Servings*: /~4/
+ *Cost per Serving*: /~$2.25/
+ *Total Cost*: /~$9.00/

* Ingredients
** Wet Mix
+ [ ] 1 can butter beans (drained and rinsed)
+ [ ] 300 g tofu (soft or firm)
+ [ ] 1 tsp vegan chicken seasoning
+ [ ] 1 tsp garlic powder
+ [ ] 1 tsp dried thyme
+ [ ] 1 tsp onion powder
+ [ ] 2 tsp white miso paste

** Dry Mix
+ [ ] 300 g rice flour (sieved)
+ [ ] 1 tsp baking powder

** Dressing
+ [ ] 2 tbsp olive oil
+ [ ] 1 tsp vegan chicken seasoning
+ [ ] 1 tsp smoked paprika
+ [ ] 1 tsp garlic powder
+ [ ] 1 tsp onion powder
+ [ ] 1 tsp hot sauce
+ [ ] salt (to taste)
+ [ ] black pepper (to taste)

** Sauce
+ [ ] 350 ml soy milk
+ [ ] 250 ml coconut cream
+ [ ] 1 tsp garlic powder
+ [ ] 1 tsp Italian seasoning
+ [ ] 1 tsp onion powder
+ [ ] 1 tsp hot sauce
+ [ ] 1 garlic clove (minced)
+ [ ] fresh sage (chopped)
+ [ ] fresh parsley (chopped)
+ [ ] salt (to taste)
+ [ ] black pepper (to taste)

** Slurry
+ [ ] 150 ml soy milk
+ [ ] 1 tbsp potato flour

* Method
** Cutlets
1. Blend the butter beans, tofu, vegan chicken seasoning, garlic powder, thyme, onion powder, and miso paste until smooth.
2. Combine the blended mixture with the sieved rice flour and baking powder to form a dough.
3. Shape the dough into cutlets.
4. Place cutlets on oiled foil, wrap lightly, and bake at 180°C for about 25 minutes.

** Pan Finish
5. Mix olive oil, vegan chicken seasoning, smoked paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, hot sauce, salt, and pepper.
6. Heat a pan over medium-high heat and add the dressing.
7. Cook the cutlets in the pan, coating evenly, until golden and lightly charred on both sides.
8. Remove the cutlets from the pan.

** Sauce
9. In the same pan, add soy milk and coconut cream.
10. Stir in garlic powder, Italian seasoning, onion powder, hot sauce, minced garlic, sage, parsley, salt, and pepper.
11. Mix the soy milk and potato flour, then add to the pan.
12. Simmer on low heat for 5–7 minutes until thickened.
13. Return cutlets to the pan and coat with sauce.

* Notes
+ Coconut cream works well as a substitute for soy cream and adds richness.
+ Medium-high heat helps achieve a slight char on the cutlets.
+ Garnish with fresh parsley, chives, or green onions before serving.
+ Serve with pasta, rice, or crusty bread.
