#+title: Pho
#+INCLUDE: /home/joshua/org/blog/recipes/header.org
+ *Prep Time*: /~20 min/
+ *Cook Time*: /~60 min/
+ *Servings*: /~4/
+ *Cost per Serving*: /~$5.50/
+ *Total Cost*: /~$22.00/

* Ingredients
** Aromatics & Spices (Broth)
- [ ] 2 tbsp neutral oil
- [ ] 4 star anise pods
- [ ] 2 cinnamon sticks
- [ ] 8 whole cloves
- [ ] 1 tsp coriander seeds
- [ ] 1 tsp black peppercorns
- [ ] 1 onion (quartered)
- [ ] 1 carrot (chunked)
- [ ] 1 celery stalk (chunked)
- [ ] 3 garlic cloves (smashed)
- [ ] 2 thumb-size pieces ginger (sliced)
- [ ] 2 pieces kombu / seaweed (4–5 inch)
- [ ] 5 oz dried mushrooms (shiitake or mix)
- [ ] 1 tbsp miso paste
- [ ] 1 tbsp soy sauce
- [ ] 7 1/2 cups water
- [ ] 1 vegetable bouillon cube
- [ ] 1 tsp kosher salt
- [ ] 1 tsp sugar

** Toppings & Noodles
- [ ] 4 servings flat rice noodles (cooked per package)
- [ ] 1 block firm tofu (pressed, sliced)
- [ ] 9 oz fresh shiitake mushrooms (sliced)
- [ ] 7 oz bok choy (halved)
- [ ] 1 bunch green onions (sliced)
- [ ] 1/2 oz fresh mint (leaves picked)
- [ ] 1/2 oz fresh cilantro (sprigs)
- [ ] 1 red chili (thinly sliced)
- [ ] 1 tbsp sesame seeds

* Method
** Broth
1. [ ] Heat oil in a pot; toast star anise, cinnamon, cloves, coriander, and peppercorns 1 minute until fragrant.
2. [ ] Add onion, carrot, celery; cook on high to char edges. Add garlic and ginger; cook 2 minutes.
3. [ ] Add kombu, dried mushrooms, miso, bouillon, water, soy, salt, and sugar. Bring to a boil; simmer 45–60 minutes. Strain and keep hot.

** Toppings
1. [ ] Pan-fry tofu until golden; season with a splash of soy sauce.
2. [ ] Sauté fresh shiitake until browned; season lightly.
3. [ ] Steam bok choy with a splash of water until tender.
4. [ ] Cook rice noodles per package; drain.

** Serve
1. [ ] Divide noodles among bowls. Ladle hot broth over.
2. [ ] Top with tofu, mushrooms, bok choy, herbs, green onions, chili, and sesame seeds.

* Notes
- [ ] Add a squeeze of lime and a dash of chili oil at the table.
- [ ] For deeper flavor, char onion and ginger under the broiler first.
