#+title: Italian Lentil Soup
#+INCLUDE: /home/joshua/org/blog/recipes/header.org
+ *Prep Time*: /~10 min/
+ *Cook Time*: /~55 min/
+ *Servings*: /~8/
+ *Cost per Serving*: /~$1.00/
+ *Total Cost*: /~$8.00/

* Ingredients
- [ ] 1/4 cup extra virgin olive oil
- [ ] 1 medium onion (diced)
- [ ] 2 ribs celery (diced, leaves included if possible)
- [ ] 2 medium carrots (diced)
- [ ] 3 oz tomato paste
- [ ] 1 cup crushed plum tomatoes
- [ ] 1 tsp dried oregano
- [ ] 1 lb brown or green lentils (rinsed)
- [ ] 8 cups water (or vegetable stock)
- [ ] 1/2 lb baby spinach (roughly chopped)
- [ ] Salt and black pepper (to taste)

* Method
1. [ ] Warm oil over medium-low; cook onion, celery, and carrots until translucent, ~10 minutes.
2. [ ] Stir in tomato paste; cook 5 minutes, stirring.
3. [ ] Add tomatoes, oregano, lentils, and water; bring to a boil, then simmer 45–50 minutes.
4. [ ] Stir in spinach to wilt; season to taste.

* Notes
- A splash of red wine vinegar at the end brightens flavor.
- Use vegetable stock for a richer broth.
