#+title: Tofu Salmon
#+INCLUDE: /home/joshua/org/blog/recipes/header.org
+ *Prep Time*: /~20 min/
+ *Cook Time*: /~12 min/
+ *Servings*: /~4/
+ *Cost per Serving*: /~$1.50/
+ *Total Cost*: /~$5.50/

* Ingredients
** Tofu
- [ ] 1 block extra-firm tofu (pressed, sliced; see notes)

** Marinade
- [ ] 1 cup vegetable broth
- [ ] 1 sheet nori (torn)
- [ ] 3 tbsp apple cider vinegar
- [ ] 1 small raw beet (peeled, chunked)
- [ ] 2 tbsp white miso paste
- [ ] 1 tsp garlic powder
- [ ] 1 tsp salt
- [ ] 1/2 tsp turmeric

** To Cook
- [ ] 1 sheet nori (as skin; optional, cut to fit)
- [ ] 1/2 cup cornstarch
- [ ] 2 tbsp light oil (for frying)
- [ ] Lemon wedges (to serve)

* Method
** Marinate
1. [ ] Blend broth, nori, vinegar, beet, miso, garlic powder, salt, and turmeric until smooth.
2. [ ] Submerge prepped tofu in marinade 30 minutes or overnight.

** Fry
1. [ ] Press a nori strip onto the back of each tofu piece (optional).
2. [ ] Dredge tofu in cornstarch. Pan-fry in hot oil 2–4 minutes per side until golden.
3. [ ] Serve hot with lemon wedges.

* Notes
- [ ] Slice tofu with shallow crosswise cuts to create a flaky texture before marinating.
- [ ] Air fry at 400°F (200°C) 12–15 minutes for a lighter option.
