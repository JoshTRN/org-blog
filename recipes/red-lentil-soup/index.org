#+title: Red Lentil Soup
#+INCLUDE: /home/joshua/org/blog/recipes/header.org
+ *Prep Time*: /~15 min/
+ *Cook Time*: /~25 min/
+ *Servings*: /~6/
+ *Cost per Serving*: /~$1.25/
+ *Total Cost*: /~$6.75/

* Ingredients
- [ ] 2 cups red lentils (rinsed, drained)
- [ ] 2 large russet potatoes (peeled, small dice)
- [ ] 1 carrot (finely diced)
- [ ] 1 onion (finely diced)
- [ ] 3 cloves garlic (minced)
- [ ] 1 tomato (diced)
- [ ] 7 cups hot water (or vegetable broth)
- [ ] 3 tbsp olive oil
- [ ] 2 tsp ground cumin
- [ ] 2 tsp kosher salt
- [ ] 1/4 tsp black pepper
- [ ] Za’atar (to taste)
- [ ] Lemon wedges (to serve)
- [ ] Parsley (chopped, to serve)

* Method
1. [ ] Sauté onion in oil 3–4 minutes; add carrot 2 minutes; add garlic 1 minute.
2. [ ] Stir in cumin, salt, pepper; add lentils and tomato; cook 1 minute.
3. [ ] Add potatoes and hot water; bring to a boil, then simmer 20 minutes.
4. [ ] Blend a portion for creaminess and return to pot. Adjust seasoning. Serve with lemon, parsley, olive oil, and za’atar.

* Notes
- Partially blending gives body while keeping texture.
- Finish with a drizzle of olive oil.
