#+title: Creamy Roasted Tomato And Garlic Pasta
#+INCLUDE: /home/joshua/org/blog/recipes/header.org
+ *Prep Time*: /~10 min/
+ *Cook Time*: /~30 min/
+ *Servings*: /~4/
+ *Cost per Serving*: /~$3.00/
+ *Total Cost*: /~$12.25/

* Ingredients
- [ ] 3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- [ ] 4 cups cherry tomatoes (whole)
- [ ] 6–10 cloves garlic (peeled, whole)
- [ ] 1 tbsp Italian seasoning
- [ ] 1 tsp kosher salt
- [ ] 1/2 tsp black pepper (freshly ground)
- [ ] 1/2 cup vegan cream cheese (softened)
- [ ] 1 lb pasta of choice (cook al dente)
- [ ] 1/2 cup fresh basil (chopped)
- [ ] 1/2 cup vegan parmesan (grated)

* Method
** Roast Tomatoes & Garlic
1. [ ] Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C).
2. [ ] Toss cherry tomatoes, garlic, olive oil, Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper on a sheet pan.
3. [ ] Roast 20–30 minutes until tomatoes burst and garlic is tender.

** Sauce
1. [ ] Blend 3/4 of the roasted tomatoes with all roasted garlic, vegan cream cheese, and half the basil until smooth; adjust salt and pepper.

** Pasta & Combine
1. [ ] Boil pasta in salted water until al dente; reserve 1 cup pasta water and drain.
2. [ ] Warm sauce in a large pan; fold in remaining roasted tomatoes.
3. [ ] Add pasta and toss; splash in reserved water as needed for a silky sauce.
4. [ ] Serve topped with vegan parmesan and remaining basil.

* Notes
- [ ] Add a pinch of red pepper flakes for gentle heat.
- [ ] For extra richness, whisk 1 tbsp olive oil into the sauce off heat.
- [ ] Use gluten-free pasta if desired.
