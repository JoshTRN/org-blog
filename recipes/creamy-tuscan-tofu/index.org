#+title: Creamy Tuscan Tofu
#+INCLUDE: /home/joshua/org/blog/recipes/header.org
+ *Prep Time*: /~15 min/
+ *Cook Time*: /~25 min/
+ *Servings*: /~2/
+ *Cost per Serving*: /~$3.75/
+ *Total Cost*: /~$7.25/

* Ingredients
** Tofu
- [ ] 1 block firm tofu (pressed, torn into chunks)
- [ ] 1 tbsp cornstarch
- [ ] 1 tbsp olive oil

** Sauce
- [ ] 1/2 medium onion (diced)
- [ ] 2 cloves garlic (minced)
- [ ] 1 cup cherry tomatoes (halved)
- [ ] 1/4 tsp salt
- [ ] 1 tsp Italian seasoning
- [ ] 1 tbsp vegan cream cheese
- [ ] 1/3 cup tomato purée
- [ ] 1/3 cup water
- [ ] 1 handful fresh spinach (roughly chopped)

** Serving
- [ ] 1 cup uncooked white rice (rinsed)

* Method
** Tofu
1. [ ] Toss tofu with cornstarch; pan-fry in oil until golden on both sides. Set aside.

** Sauce
1. [ ] Sauté onion and garlic until fragrant. Add tomatoes and salt; cook until softened.
2. [ ] Stir in Italian seasoning, vegan cream cheese, tomato purée, and water; simmer 3 minutes to thicken.
3. [ ] Return tofu and fold in spinach until just wilted.

** Rice
1. [ ] Cook rice with 1 1/2 cups water until tender; rest covered 5 minutes. Serve with creamy tofu.

* Notes
- [ ] Air fry tofu at 400°F (200°C) 12–15 minutes for extra crispness.
