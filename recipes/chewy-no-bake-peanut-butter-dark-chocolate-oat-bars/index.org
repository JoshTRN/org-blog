#+title: Chewy No-Bake Peanut Butter Dark Chocolate Oat Bars
#+INCLUDE: /home/joshua/org/blog/recipes/header.org
+ *Prep Time*: /~15 min/
+ *Cook Time*: /~0 min/
+ *Servings*: /~12/
+ *Cost per Serving*: /~$0.50/
+ *Total Cost*: /~$5.00/

* Ingredients
- [ ] 1 cup rolled oats
- [ ] 1 cup puffed rice cereal
- [ ] 1/2 cup natural peanut butter
- [ ] 1/3 cup maple syrup
- [ ] 1 tsp vanilla extract
- [ ] 1/4 tsp fine salt
- [ ] 1/2 cup vegan dark chocolate chips
- [ ] 1 tbsp coconut oil

* Method
1. [ ] Line an 8 inch square pan with parchment. Mix oats and puffed rice in a bowl.
2. [ ] Warm peanut butter, maple syrup, vanilla, and salt on low until smooth; pour over dry mix and combine.
3. [ ] Press into pan firmly. Melt chocolate with coconut oil; spread over top.
4. [ ] Refrigerate 1 hour until set; slice into bars.

* Notes
- Add 2 tbsp chopped roasted peanuts for crunch.
- Store chilled up to 1 week or freeze.
