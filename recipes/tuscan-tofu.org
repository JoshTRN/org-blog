
* Cutlets
** Wet mix
1 Can Butter Beans
300g tofu looks silken or firm
1 tsp Chicken seasoning
1 tsp garlic powder
1 tsp dried thyme
1 tsp onion powder
2 tsp (white?) miso paste

Blend all of the above

** dry mix

300g rice flour
1 tsp baking powder

seive into bowl
mix with liquid cutlet mix to form a dough
shape dough into cutlets

line tin foil with evoo and wrap cutlets
bake about 25 min at 180 c

** Dressing

2 tbsp olive oil
1 tsp chicken seasoning
1 tsp smoked prprika
1 tsp garlic powder
1 tsp onion powder
Salt & pepper
1 tsp hot sauce (probably go with frank's)

mix all together

Transfer cutlets to pan
dress evenly with dressing cooking until golden brown very slightly chared on both sides (maybe medium-high heat?)
remove cutlets


** Sauce
in the same pan, add 350 ml of soy milk
250 ml soy cream (I'll use coconut cream. Don't have soy cream)
add in 1 tsp garlic powder
add in 1 tsp italian seasonin
add in 1 tsp onion powder
add in 1 tsp hot sauce
stir until evenly
fresh sage (eyeball)
fresh parsely (eyeball)
1 clove garlic
black pepper
salt
stir again

whisk together 150ml soy milk and 1 tbsp of potato flour
add it to the sauce mix

Simmer on low heat 5-7 minutes until thickened

garnish with fresh parsley (chives, or green onions)
